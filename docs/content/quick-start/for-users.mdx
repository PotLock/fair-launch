# Quick Start - For Users

<Callout type="info">
Ready to launch or participate in token launches on POTLAUNCH? This guide will get you started in minutes!
</Callout>

## What You'll Need

- A Solana wallet (Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare)
- Some SOL for transaction fees
- Your token idea ready to launch!

## Getting Started

<Steps>

### Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit [potlaunch.com](https://potlaunch.com/)
2. Click "Connect Wallet" in the top right corner
3. Select your preferred wallet (Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare)
4. Approve the connection request

### Launch Your Token

1. Click the "Launch Token" button
2. Fill in your token details:
   - Token name and symbol
   - Description and social links
   - Upload your token logo
3. Configure your launch settings:
   - Choose your launch mechanism (Bonding Curve or Dutch Auction)
   - Set initial pricing parameters
   - Configure safety features
4. Review and confirm your launch

### Participate in Launches

1. Browse active token launches on the homepage
2. Click on any token to view details
3. Buy tokens directly through the interface
4. Track your holdings in your dashboard

</Steps>

## Key Features for Users

### Token Launch

- **Easy Creation** - Launch tokens in just a few clicks
- **Multiple Launch Types** - Choose from bonding curves, Dutch auctions, or fair launches
- **Customizable** - Configure pricing, fees, and safety parameters

### Safety Features

- **Anti-Rug Protection** - Built-in mechanisms to protect against rug pulls
- **Transparent Pricing** - All pricing is on-chain and visible
- **Liquidity Lock** - Automatic liquidity locking for security

### Cross-Chain Bridge

- **Multi-Chain Support** - Bridge tokens across Solana, NEAR, and other chains
- **Fast Transfers** - Quick and secure cross-chain transfers
- **Low Fees** - Competitive bridging fees
