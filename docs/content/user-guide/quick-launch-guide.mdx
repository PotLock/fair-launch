# User Guide

## Quick Launch Your Token on POTLAUNCH

Get your token live in minutes with POTLAUNCH's Quick Mint feature. This guide will walk you through the fastest way to create and launch your token on Solana.

<Callout type="info">
Quick Mint is perfect for meme coins and simple token launches. If you need advanced features like custom tokenomics, vesting schedules, or bonding curves, check out the [Advanced Launch Settings](/user-guide/advanced-launch-settings) guide.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

POTLAUNCH offers two token creation methods:

### Quick Mint (Recommended)
- **Launch Time:** 2-5 minutes
- **Best For:** Meme coins, simple tokens, quick launches
- **Features:**
  - Standard meme coin setup with proven defaults
  - 1 billion token supply (6 decimals)
  - All tokens mint directly to your wallet
  - Skip complex tokenomics configuration

### Custom Creation (Advanced)
- **Launch Time:** 10-20 minutes
- **Best For:** Projects requiring custom tokenomics
- **Features:**
  - Complete tokenomics customization
  - Token allocations and vesting schedules
  - Bonding curve and fee configuration
  - Advanced authority and security settings

**This guide covers Quick Mint.** For Custom Creation, see [Advanced Launch Settings](/user-guide/advanced-launch-settings).

## Getting Started

<Steps>

### Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit [POTLAUNCH Create Page](https://potlaunch.com/create)
2. Click **Connect Wallet** button in the top right
3. Select your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.)
4. Approve the connection request

<Callout type="warning">
Make sure you have enough SOL in your wallet for:
- Token creation fees (~0.01-0.05 SOL)
- Transaction fees
- Initial liquidity (if applicable)
</Callout>

### Choose Quick Mint

After connecting your wallet, you'll see two options:
- **Quick Mint** (Recommended) - Simple, fast token creation
- **Custom Creation** (Advanced) - Full control over tokenomics

Click on the **Quick Mint** card to select it, then click **Continue to Token Creation**.

### Fill in Token Information

Now you'll enter your token's basic details:

</Steps>

## Token Information

### Required Fields

#### Token Name
- Enter your token's full name (e.g., "Dogecoin", "Pepe Token")
- This is how your token will appear everywhere
- Can be any length, but keep it memorable

**Example:** `My Awesome Token`

#### Token Symbol
- Enter a 1-5 character ticker symbol (e.g., "DOGE", "PEPE")
- Automatically converts to uppercase
- Maximum 5 characters

**Example:** `MAT`

#### Token Logo
Upload your token's logo image:
1. Click the **Token Logo** upload area
2. Select an image file (JPG, PNG, GIF)
3. **Recommended size:** 512x512 pixels
4. Wait for upload to complete

<Callout type="tip">
Use a square image with transparent background for best results. Your logo will be displayed on token listings, trading interfaces, and wallets.
</Callout>

#### Banner Image
Upload a banner for your token page:
1. Click the **Banner Image** upload area
2. Select an image file (JPG, PNG, GIF)
3. **Recommended size:** 1500x500 pixels
4. Wait for upload to complete

### Optional Fields (Recommended)

#### Description
- Describe your token's purpose or use case
- Help potential investors understand your project
- Can include emojis and formatting

**Example:**
```
The ultimate meme token for the community! 🚀
Join us on our mission to the moon.
```

#### Tags
Categorize your token for better discoverability:
1. Click **Add Tags** button
2. Select relevant categories:
   - Meme
   - DeFi
   - NFT
   - Gaming
   - Community
   - And more...
3. Multiple tags can be selected
4. Click **Confirm** when done

#### Social Links

Add your project's social media (automatically formatted):

**Website**
- Enter your domain: `yourwebsite.com`
- System adds `https://` automatically

**X/Twitter**
- Enter your username: `yourusername`
- System adds `x.com/` prefix automatically

**Telegram**
- Enter your channel: `yourchannel`
- System adds `t.me/` prefix automatically

## Quick Mint Defaults

When you use Quick Mint, these settings are automatically configured:

### Token Supply
- **Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 tokens (1 billion)
- **Decimals:** 6 (standard for Solana tokens)
- **Quote Token:** SOL (wrapped SOL)

### Token Distribution
- **100% to Creator:** All tokens mint directly to your connected wallet
- **No Vesting:** All tokens immediately available
- **No Locks:** Full control from day one

### Token Standard
- **Type:** SPL Token (Solana Program Library)
- **Network:** Solana Mainnet
- **Authority:** Creator wallet (you)

### Fees
- **Creation Fee:** Standard POTLAUNCH creation fee
- **No Trading Fees:** Simple token, no bonding curve fees
- **No Vesting Fees:** No vesting configured

## Review and Deploy

<Steps>

### Review Your Token Details

Before deploying, review all information:

**Token Information:**
- ✅ Token Name is correct
- ✅ Token Symbol is correct (max 5 chars)
- ✅ Logo image uploaded and looks good
- ✅ Banner image uploaded and looks good
- ✅ Description is clear (if added)
- ✅ Tags are relevant (if added)
- ✅ Social links are correct (if added)

**Token Settings:**
- ✅ Supply: 1 billion tokens
- ✅ Decimals: 6
- ✅ All tokens to your wallet
- ✅ Quote Token: SOL

### Deploy Your Token

1. Click the **Deploy Token** button
2. Review the transaction details in your wallet
3. Confirm you have enough SOL for fees
4. Approve the transaction
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation (usually 5-30 seconds)

<Callout type="success">
Congratulations! Your token is now live on Solana. You'll receive a confirmation with your token's mint address.
</Callout>

### After Deployment

Once your token is deployed:

1. **Token Address** - Copy and save your token's mint address
2. **View Token** - Click to see your token page on POTLAUNCH
3. **Share Link** - Share your token page with your community
4. **Add to Wallet** - Import token to your wallet using the mint address

</Steps>

## Adding Liquidity (Optional)

After creating your token, you may want to add liquidity to enable trading:

### Why Add Liquidity?

- Enables others to buy/sell your token
- Creates price discovery
- Increases token utility
- Builds trust with holders

### How to Add Liquidity

<Steps>

### Choose a DEX

Popular Solana DEXes:
- **Raydium** - Most popular, high liquidity
- **Orca** - User-friendly interface
- **Meteora** - Dynamic liquidity pools

### Prepare Your Tokens

Make sure you have:
- Your minted tokens in your wallet
- SOL for liquidity pairing
- Extra SOL for transaction fees

### Create Liquidity Pool

1. Go to your chosen DEX
2. Find "Create Pool" or "Add Liquidity"
3. Select your token and SOL
4. Set initial price ratio
5. Add liquidity amounts
6. Confirm transaction

<Callout type="warning">
**Important:** When creating a liquidity pool:
- Start with reasonable liquidity (don't put all tokens)
- Set a fair initial price
- Consider locking LP tokens to build trust
- Understand impermanent loss risks
</Callout>

</Steps>

## Trading Your Token

### Buying Tokens

Anyone can buy your token if liquidity exists:

1. Go to the token page on POTLAUNCH or a DEX
2. Click **Buy** or **Swap**
3. Enter amount to purchase
4. Review price and slippage
5. Approve transaction
6. Tokens arrive in wallet

### Selling Tokens

Token holders can sell:

1. Navigate to trading interface
2. Click **Sell**
3. Enter amount to sell
4. Review price (may include slippage)
5. Approve transaction
6. SOL arrives in wallet

### Understanding Slippage

- **Slippage** = Difference between expected and actual price
- **Higher slippage** = Less liquidity or larger trade
- **Recommended:** Start with 1-5% slippage
- **Adjust** if transaction fails

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Upload Failed**
- File too large (max 5MB)
- Unsupported format (use JPG, PNG, GIF)
- Poor internet connection
- **Solution:** Compress image, check format, retry upload

**Transaction Failed**
- Insufficient SOL for fees
- Wallet not connected
- Network congestion
- **Solution:** Add SOL, reconnect wallet, increase fee, retry

**Token Not Showing**
- Blockchain confirmation pending
- Wallet cache issue
- Need to manually import
- **Solution:** Wait 1-2 minutes, refresh, import via mint address

**Can't Add Liquidity**
- Don't have tokens in wallet
- Insufficient SOL
- Wrong DEX network
- **Solution:** Verify token balance, add SOL, check network

## Security Tips

### Wallet Security

1. **Never Share Seed Phrase**
   - Keep it private and secure
   - Write it down offline
   - Never screenshot or type online

2. **Use Hardware Wallet**
   - For large amounts
   - Maximum security
   - Ledger or similar

3. **Verify Transactions**
   - Always review before signing
   - Check addresses carefully
   - Confirm amounts

### Project Security

1. **Use Official Links**
   - Verify POTLAUNCH domain
   - Don't click suspicious links
   - Bookmark official site

2. **Beware of Scams**
   - No one will DM you first for "support"
   - Don't share wallet credentials
   - Verify before connecting wallet

3. **Smart Contract Risks**
   - Understand token creation risks
   - Start with small amounts for testing
   - Research before investing heavily

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to create a token?

Quick Mint costs approximately:
- **Creation Fee:** 0.01-0.05 SOL (varies)
- **Transaction Fees:** ~0.001 SOL
- **Total:** Usually less than 0.1 SOL

### Can I change token details after creation?

With Quick Mint:
- **Can Change:** Social links, description (off-chain metadata)
- **Cannot Change:** Token name, symbol, supply, decimals (on-chain)
- **Recommendation:** Double-check everything before deploying

### Do all tokens go to my wallet?

Yes! With Quick Mint:
- 100% of supply mints to your connected wallet
- No vesting or locks
- Immediate full control
- You decide distribution

### How do I enable trading?

To enable trading:
1. Create liquidity pool on a DEX
2. Pair your token with SOL
3. Set initial price
4. Add liquidity
5. Others can now trade

### What happens if I lose my wallet?

If you lose access to your wallet:
- You lose control of your tokens
- Cannot update token authority
- Tokens in wallet are inaccessible
- **Prevention:** Backup seed phrase securely

### Can I use Quick Mint for serious projects?

Quick Mint is great for:
- Meme coins
- Community tokens
- Simple utility tokens
- Experimental projects

Consider Custom Creation for:
- Projects with investors
- Complex tokenomics needed
- Vesting schedules required
- Bonding curve mechanisms
